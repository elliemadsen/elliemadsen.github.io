Ellie Madsen is a NYC-based researcher, software engineer, and designer working at the intersection of media, computation, and the environment. She is interested in digital representations of the Earth, including geographic information systems, climate models, and spatial data visualization.

Ellie is currently a Research Assistant at the Center for Climate Systems Research and a Research Scholar at the Buell Center for the Study of American Architecture.

Ellie holds degrees in Computer Science and Environmental Studies from Brown University and a Master's in Computational Design Practices from Columbia University GSAPP.

You can find Ellie on GitHub, Are.na, LinkedIn, and email.
