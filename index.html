Ellie Madsen is a NYC-based researcher, engineer, and designer working at the intersection of media, computation, and the environment.


Her research practice questions the spatial and aesthetic conditions of digital representations of Earth, such as geographic information systems, climate models, and remotely sensed data. Her research methods include environmental science, computational design, mapping, modeling, writing, data visualization, software engineering, semantic analysis, and web development.

Ellie is currently a Research Assistant at the Climate Impacts Group and a Research Scholar at the Buell Center for the Study of American Architecture.

You can find Ellie on GitHub, Are.na, LinkedIn, and email.
