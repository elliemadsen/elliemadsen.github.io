At the Center for Climate Systems Research, co-located between Columbia Climate School and NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies, I assist with researching Analytical Protocols for Hydrological Vulnerability Assessment and BMP Targeting in the Cannonsville Reservoir Watershed, one of the most critical sub-basins of New York City's water supply system. My contributions include various geographic data analyses, primarily downscaling global sets of sparse GCM (General Climate Model) outputs to a higher resolution across the watershed bounds by applying the delta change factor methodology to observational baseline data for temperature, precipitation, evapotranspiration, and leaf area index. I created this web visualization tool as a way to overlay multiple data layers (e.g. individual CDL crops over bivariate temperature and precipitation projections), enrich scientists' three-dimensional mental map of the watershed, and communicate results to non-technical stakeholders.
