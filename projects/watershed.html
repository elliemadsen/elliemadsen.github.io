At the Climate Impacts Group, co-located between Columbia University Center for Climate Systems Research and NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies, I research vulnerability and resiliency of the Cannonsville Watershed, the most critical source of New York City's water supply system. I contribute various geographic data analyses, including downscaling global sparse NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 climate model data to understand projected local impacts at a high resolution, and quantifying temporal autocorrelation of biomass water content as a proxy for resiliency to inform detection of ecosystemic critical slowing down.

To supplement this work, I created a web visualization that overlays various data layers (e.g. individual CDL crops over bivariate temperature and precipitation projections) in order to enrich scientists' mental maps of the watershed and communicate results to non-technical stakeholders.
